    About the hazardous-area rings

    Rings around shakers, the wellhead, mud tanks and fuel tanks stand in for Zone 1 (inner) and Zone 2 (outer) areas. Their radii are illustrative. The real boundaries come from the site's hazardous-area classification study (API RP 505 or IEC 60079-10-1), and any camera inside them must carry a matching Ex rating. The checks flag non-Ex cameras placed inside a ring.

    The coverage model includes mount height, pitch, field of view, sensor resolution, 416 px detector letterboxing and tiling, steep-view foreshortening, and the 8 m drill floor as its own surface. It leaves out blocking by equipment, dust, heat shimmer and glare.

    Twin file

    The whole twin: layout, cameras, zones, rules and their switches, and assumptions. Copy it to keep a version, or paste one back to load it. The structure maps directly onto a domain plugin if you bring this into your digital-twin platform later.

    Notebook config

    One SCENES entry per camera, with every covered zone moved into that camera's frame, up to six component corners for point calibration (replace the predicted pixels with the ones you measure), plus the rule switches for the notebook's Rule Studio engine (§7b). Paste after the notebook cell, set CFG.scene and CFG.source, then call run_source().
